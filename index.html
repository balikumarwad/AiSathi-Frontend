A FEW THINGS, EXPLAINED

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Getting familiar should feel simple.

What is AIsathi?

AIsathi is being shaped as a local connection to AI for people and developers in Nepal. This page presents the proposed chat, API, and local payment experience.

Can I pay with eSewa or Khalti?

eSewa and Khalti are the proposed local payment integrations. Live merchant approval and payment availability have not been verified for this frontend. This page does not accept payments.

What’s the difference between chat and the API?

Chat is a direct interface for asking questions and working with AI. An API lets a developer send requests from their own app. The available features and prices need to be confirmed for each experience.

How will my balance be used?

The proposed balance pays for model usage. The amount consumed depends on the model and request. Exact rates, bonus conditions, and refund or expiry terms must be confirmed before purchasing.

Which models can I use?

The supported model catalog is still an integration dependency. No provider logo or model count on this concept should be taken as a promise of availability.

How is my data handled?

Data handling can depend on the selected model and upstream provider. Review the published privacy policy and model-specific terms when the service is available. No privacy guarantee is made by this preview.
